For NGOs & care homes

If your organisation needs food, check the listings and contact the donor directly. Confirm the quantity, ingredients, preparation time and pickup arrangements before accepting.

Food safety matters: Accept only food that has been handled and stored safely. Do not distribute food if its safety is uncertain, and follow local food-safety guidance.

What to confirm

  • Food preparation time
  • Quantity and ingredients
  • Safe storage and transport
  • Pickup location and time
